Fathom Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Fathom is a performance tool for magicians and mentalists, created by Yair Sarig and presented by The Daily Magician. This page explains what the app does with your information. The short version: Fathom does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data.

Microphone

Fathom uses the microphone to recognise spoken numbers, dates and card names while a routine is active. Speech recognition runs entirely on your phone, offline. Audio is processed in a short in-memory buffer and is never recorded, saved to a file, or sent anywhere. The app does not have internet permission, so it cannot send audio or anything else off your device.

Photos and camera

If you add your own pictures to a routine, you choose them with your phone's photo picker or take them with your phone's camera app. The pictures are copied into Fathom's private storage on your phone so the routine can show them. They are never uploaded or shared.

Sensors and vibration

Some routines read the phone's motion sensor to notice when the phone is placed face down or put in a pocket, and use the vibration motor to give you cues. This happens on the phone only; nothing is stored or sent.

Your settings and routines

Your stacks, saved decks, lists, shows and settings are stored only on your phone. They are not included in cloud backups. Uninstalling Fathom deletes them.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • No data sold or shared with third parties.
  • No location, contacts or other personal data accessed.

Children

Fathom is intended for adults and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
